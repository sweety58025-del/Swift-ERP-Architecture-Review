Purpose of this checklist

Use this checklist during the engineering review to establish the current state, understand design decisions and trade-offs, identify material risks, and agree on a practical target architecture. The checklist does not prescribe a specific technology; engineering decisions should be justified against business requirements, expected scale and operational risk.

Required Review Deliverables

Where applicable, the engineering team should provide or walk through the following artifacts during the review.

Current-state architecture diagram
Proposed / target architecture and evolution path
Data architecture overview, including major collections and indexes
API and external integration architecture
Deployment, environment and infrastructure overview
Security and RBAC overview
Backup, recovery and operational resilience approach
Known risks and technical-debt register with proposed priorities

Expected Review Outcome

  • Confirm the actual current architecture and technology stack.
  • Identify architectural limitations, security risks and operational risks.
  • Establish the practical target architecture and evolution path.
  • Separate immediate requirements from requirements that become necessary at a defined scale.
  • Record technical decisions, owners, priorities and follow-up actions.
